a little note, from me.

hey, you. ♡

aku mau minta maaf, ya, for the things i said or did that might have hurt you. mungkin ada cara aku nyampein sesuatu yang kurang tepat, atau ada hal yang belum bisa aku pahamin dari sudut pandang kamu.

i'm not saying this just to make things okay again. aku sadar kalau niat baik aja sometimes isn't enough; cara kita memperlakukan dan memahami satu sama lain juga matters.

aku masih belajar buat communicate better, buat dengerin tanpa buru-buru defensif, dan buat ga selalu ngeliat sesuatu dari sisi aku aja. aku ga bisa janji bakal selalu perfect, but i can try to be more aware of my actions.

kamu ga harus langsung ngerasa baik-baik aja, and you don't have to pretend that nothing happened. take your time. aku cuma pengen kamu tau kalau perasaan kamu tetap penting dan layak didengerin.

thank you for the little things, for your time, and for moments that might seem ordinary but quietly mean a lot to me.

semoga kita bisa belajar dari hal-hal yang ga selalu berjalan sesuai harapan, and handle them with a little more patience, honesty, and understanding.

that's all i wanted to say. no pressure, no need to find the perfect reply. i just wanted to be honest with you. ♡
